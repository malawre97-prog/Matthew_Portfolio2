Resume

My name is Matthew Lawrence. I am graphic design student at the British Columbia Institute of Technology.

I am developing my skills in visual communication, digital design, typography, layout, branding, and advertising.

I enjoy taking an idea and turning it into a visual concept that communicates a message clearly. I am especially interested in how typography, colour, imagery, and layout can work together to create a strong design.

Throughout my studies, I have been learning how to use professional design software and how to approach projects from both a creative and practical perspective.

My Goals

My goal is to continue developing my design skills and create professional - quality visual communication projects.

I will join the First Nations Technology Council after I graduate and receive my graphic design diploma, to upgrade my graphic design skills further.

Eventually I want to become UI-UX designer that incorporates AI into my designs to have great advantage within the competitive graphic design space.

Another goal of mine is to be a jack of all trades so to speak, that way I can have ability to work on variety projects in my future career.

My Design Interests

My Interests include several areas of graphic design. I enjoy exploring different forms of themes whether it's pirates, mid evil age, Future, space, comic strips,videogames,marvel, and so many more... When it comes to comic strips i'm intersted in learning to use drawing tablet to incorporate into my future designs.

Branding:

I am intersted in creating logos, visual identities, packaging, and other elements that help a brand develop recognizable personality.

Typography:

Typography is an important part of my design process. I enjoy experimenting with typefaces, hierarchy, spacing, and composition to make information easier to understand.

Advertising:

I enjoy developing advertising concepts that combbine imagery and typography to communicate an idea quickly and effectively.

Digital Design:

I am also intersted in digital interfaces and designing experinces that are clear, accesible, and easy to navigate.

Design Approach:

My approach to design begins with understanding the purpose of the project and the audience it is intended for

From there, I explore ideas through research, sketches, typography, colour, imagery, and layout. I belive that strong design should not only look intersting but should also communicate its message effectively.

I also believe that experimentaion is an important part of the creative process. Trying different layouts, compositions, and visual directions can help reveal stronger solutions.

Colour:

Colour in my opinion is one of if not the most influential design element when it comes to influencing human emotion, brand identity, visual hierarchy, guides user attention, improves accesibility, and creates visual harmony.

Conclusion:

I am a passionate and motivated grapic designer who enjoys turning ideas into meaningful and visually engaging designs. I am continously developing my skills in branding, typography, layout, digital design, and visual communication. I approach each project with creativity, attention to detail, and a willingness to learn.
